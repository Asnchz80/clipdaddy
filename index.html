ClipDaddy

Livestreams in, TikTok clips out.

ClipDaddy records the livestreams a creator chooses, finds the moments worth watching, cuts them into vertical clips with captions, and posts them to that creator's TikTok account after the creator approves each one.

Record

The creator picks the channels to follow. When one goes live, ClipDaddy records it.

Find the moment

Speech is transcribed and scored for hook, setup and payoff. Most of a stream is not a clip, and that is fine.

Cut and caption

Each clip is reframed to 9:16, following whoever is talking, with word-by-word captions and a headline.

Post with approval

The creator connects their TikTok account with Login Kit. Every post is reviewed on a posting screen where the creator writes the caption, chooses who can watch, and confirms. Clips can also be sent to the creator's TikTok inbox to finish in the TikTok app.
