Privacy Policy

Effective 26 September 2026

ClipDaddy is software a creator runs to turn livestreams into short clips and post them to the creator's own TikTok account. This policy explains what ClipDaddy stores when you connect a TikTok account, and what it does with it.

What ClipDaddy receives from TikTok

When you connect your TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, ClipDaddy receives an access token and a refresh token for the permissions you approve, plus your TikTok display name, username and avatar. The permissions are: user.info.basic (to show which account is connected), video.upload (to send a clip to your TikTok inbox) and video.publish (to post a clip you have approved).

What ClipDaddy stores

  • Your TikTok tokens, kept in a file readable only by the operating-system account that runs ClipDaddy. They are never written to logs, shown on screen, or sent anywhere except TikTok's API.
  • Your TikTok username and display name, to label the connected account.
  • For each clip sent to TikTok: the publish identifier TikTok returns, the status TikTok reports, the caption and settings you chose, and the time.
  • Recordings, transcripts and clips of the livestreams you chose to follow, on the machine you run ClipDaddy on.

What ClipDaddy does not do

  • It does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. There are no advertising or analytics partners.
  • It does not post to your account without your action on the posting screen, and it does not add watermarks or promotional material to your videos.
  • It does not read your TikTok inbox, messages, followers or other accounts' data.

Where data lives

ClipDaddy is self-hosted: all data stays on the computer the creator runs it on. TikTok processes the videos and captions you post under TikTok's Privacy Policy.

Disconnecting and deletion

You can disconnect at any time from ClipDaddy's dashboard, which deletes the stored tokens, or by removing ClipDaddy under Settings › Security › Apps and websites in the TikTok app, which revokes its access. Deleting the ClipDaddy data folder removes everything else.
